About GIBYT

innovation.

GIBYT is a software company building products, websites, and apps — and figuring out, in public, what a small team can ship.

Foundations & Philosophy

The engineering standards and values driving our software development studio.

Our story

GIBYT started as a simple idea: most software takes far longer to become real than it should. We're building GIBYT to close that gap — starting with small, working products and growing from there, instead of planning a big launch before anything exists.

Mission

Build software that actually ships and actually gets used — for our own products, and for the people we build with.

Vision

A studio known for turning ideas into working software quickly, without cutting corners on how that software is built.

Values

  • Ship real, working pieces — not just plans
  • Be honest about what's built and what isn't yet
  • Keep the architecture clean enough to grow
  • Learn in public, and improve in the open
